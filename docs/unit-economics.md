# Unit economics

**Date:** August 2026
**Prices:** USDA AMS terminal-market examples (Baltimore / Boston, 18–20 Aug 2026) plus typical foodservice packaging. Re-price weekly from your local terminal.

These are **planning numbers**, not a promise. Produce moves. The point of the sheet is to know which cup still works when mango jumps $4 a flat.

**Breakeven vs. $53/day fixed:** about **8 cups**. That is not the real breakeven. The real breakeven is whether the owner’s time is worth more than the next job.

At **40 cups/day**, the cart pays a modest full-time wage. At **60+**, it is a good owner-operator business. At **25**, it is a side hustle and will feel like the thin LA numbers ($50–$100/day) once you count unpaid hours.

If you **hire** a cart operator at $18/hour × 8 hours + 1 hour close = ~$160/day, you need roughly **50+ cups** before the cart clears more than the wage. Do not hire until the owner has proven 60.

## 3. Volume reality check

| Setting | Cups / day (order of magnitude) | Source of estimate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weak sidewalk, price-sensitive corner | 20–40 | LA vendor reporting; ethnography of long days / thin take |
| Farmers market, decent weather | 40–80 | Typical specialty-food booth |
| Locked weekday lunch (campus / hospital) | 50–90 | Food-truck lunch pads |
| Hot Saturday sports / festival | 100–250 | Event catering pattern |

Do not model 100 cups as the base case. Model **40**. Treat 60 as the goal. Treat 100 as an event.

### Do not buy at launch

- A food truck

## 5. What “good” looks like at day 90

- 20 days logged
- Average **≥50 cups/day** on the weekday site
- Loaded COGS **≤ $3.50**
- Cut-melon discard **< 8%** of melon bought (buy closer to the bone)
- No health mark that you cannot explain
- One written site agreement for the next quarter

If those five are true, buy nothing else except a second ice well and a Saturday event calendar.

## 6. Sensitivity (16 oz cup)

| Shock | Effect on $10 cup | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mango +$0.80/fruit | COGS +$0.27 | Drop mango from the mix that week |
| Pineapple carton +$6 | COGS +$0.17 | Smaller pineapple spear, more watermelon |
| 20% waste instead of 15% | COGS +$0.12 | Sharper buying; corer instead of knife |
| Price $8 because “street food should be cheap” | Gross −$2.00 | Wrong site. Move. Do not train the market to $8 |
| Hired labor $160/day | Need ~24 extra cups just to pay the wage | Owner stays on the cart until 60+ cups |

Price is a location decision, not a personality decision.
