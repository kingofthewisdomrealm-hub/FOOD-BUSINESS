# Visual mockup: how the cart works

Concept shots for the San Antonio launch. Rainbow umbrella and fruit on ice from Mexican street carts. Mechanical fridge, hand sink, and a 20–40 second finish in front of the customer — that is what makes it legal and repeatable.

## The cart

Private-property lunch pad (gym / hospital / campus lot). Not a sidewalk next to an existing *frutero*.

![Permitted fruit cart on a commercial lot, rainbow umbrella, ice-packed whole fruit](mockup/fruit-cart-san-antonio-concept.png)

**Kept from street carts:** loud umbrella, whole fruit packed in ice, standing spears in a clear cup, chile-lime and chamoy, stainless chassis.

**Added for a Type II unit:** mechanical fridge (cut melon at ≤41°F), integral hand sink, commissary wash/core in the morning, finish-to-order instead of packed cups sitting in the sun.

## Top layout

Customer faces the ice well. Operator works the finish edge. Fruit that is already cut lives in the fridge, not on the ice.

![Top layout: ice well, finish station, fridge, hand sink](mockup/fruit-cart-top-layout.png)

| Zone | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Ice well | The sell. Whole mango, pineapple, melon, cucumber, jícama. Theater, not the cold hold. |
| Finish station | Spear jig, pack, fresh lime, chile-lime salt, optional chamoy. 20–40 seconds. |
| Fridge | TCS hold ≤41°F. Lidded cambros, time labels, temp log. Discard leftover cut melon at close. |
| Hand sink | Pressurized hot and cold. Cutting on a folding table is a shutdown. |
| Tanks | Potable in; wastewater ≥15% larger. Dumped at the commissary every operating day. |

## The cup

16 oz mixed, about $10. Spears that stand, not grocery cubes. Lime and chile-lime always. Chamoy +$1.

![16 oz fruta picada cup with standing spears, lime, chile-lime salt](mockup/fruta-picada-16oz-cup.png)

Launch mix: watermelon, pineapple, mango, cucumber, jícama. When mango spikes, drop it that week.

## A day (hybrid)

1. **5:30** — Zarzamora terminal or SAWPM. Buy for the day.
2. **7:00** — Commissary: wash, core pineapple, peel melon, peel cucumber/jícama, push sticks, ice, load, log temps.
3. **9:30–2:30** — Lunch pad. Final spear, pack, finish, tap to pay.
4. **3:30** — Optional heat shift. One excellent lunch is enough to validate.
5. **7:30** — Commissary close: dump tanks, wash cart, throw leftover cut melon, store whole fruit.

## Tools: skin and cut

Street prices checked 20 Aug 2026 (WebstaurantStore, Tribest, manufacturer pages). Manual NSF lever tools beat factory lines for this cart. Cubers and 220V mango plants are the wrong machine.

### What to buy

| Fruit | Skin / peel | Cut into standing spears | Buy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pineapple | One-pull corer/peeler. Cylinder comes off the core and the rind in one stroke. | Stand the cylinder, trim to 4–5", push through a 1/2" stick cutter. | **Nemco 55775** 3-1/2" Easy Pineapple Corer/Peeler, NSF, USA, **$1,043**. Pair with **Nemco 55450-3** Easy FryKutter 1/2", NSF, **$417**. |
| Watermelon | Knife, not a peeler. Wash whole melon first (FDA: rind is the contamination path). Cut ends, follow the white/red line, rind off. National Watermelon Promotion Board: ~2.5–3 min, **60–70% edible yield**. | Slabs into 4–5" sticks with the same 1/2" FryKutter, or the 12" knife if the grid crushes and weeps. | **Victorinox Fibrox 12" chef** (5.2003.31-X2), NSF, **$83**. Do not buy a wedger — wedges keep the rind on. |
| Cucumber | NSF Y-peeler. ~27 cukes/day at 80 cups. A $260 stand peeler is for a plant, not cart one. | Trim to length, 1/2" FryKutter. | **Dexter-Russell or Winco NSF Y-peeler**, straight blade, **$6–12**. Serrated (Winco VP-301) if the skin is waxy. Spare in the kit. |
| Jícama | Same Y-peeler or paring knife. Thick, papery skin, like a potato. | 1/2" FryKutter. Jícama is dense; a home fry cutter will flex and a Vollrath InstaCut is **not rated for hard roots**. | Same Nemco FryKutter as pineapple. That is why the cutter is Nemco, not Vollrath. |
| Mango | No cart-scale NSF peeler that keeps yield. Factory lines (Fengxiang, Havery, 1,000+ fruit/hour, 220V) are canneries. | Cheeks off with the knife. Score in the skin, slice spears off. OXO splitters assume a small centered pit — Kent / Tommy Atkins at the terminal wander. | **Knife.** Limit mango to one spear per mixed cup if the operator is slow. Custom cheek jig after the cart is profitable. |
| Lime | None. | Halve and squeeze fresh. Optional 6-section wedger later. | Same chef knife. |

**Cheaper pineapple alternative:** Cancan TBP-801 (Tribest, Turkey) **$625**, four blade diameters. Good grocery-style peel. Weaker NSF story for a Texas plan review than Nemco. Use it only if the inspector accepts it and capital is tight.

**Do not buy:** Amazon mango gadgets; Vollrath InstaCut dice blades (cubes, and not for jícama); abrasive potato peelers; Alibaba 300 kg/h slicers; ABL/WML5 watermelon lines; any tool that outputs chips or cubes.
